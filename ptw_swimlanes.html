→
Atmosphere Within Safe Limits?
All 4 Gases Safe → Advances Hazardous Gas → Force Ventilate & Retest
Tower Incharge Section Head Direct Clearance
Step 3 · Section Clearance Clear Entry Operations Verifies emergency response team availability and clears adjacent utility lines
→ Handoff to EHS Safety

Step 4 · Statutory Review Audit Calibration & Winch Checks gas detector calibration certificate, entrant briefing log & activates permit
→
Active Permit Entrants Inside Chamber Continuous gas detection; Standby Attendant logs entrant times and maintains lifeline communication

Sunday Work (SUN) Tile & Weekend Governance Swimlane

Saturday Advance Preparation · Sunday Zero-Creation Lockout · Scheduled Daylight Closure
Weekend Operations Policy
Operational Scope: Governs weekend construction activities when head office teams are off-duty. Operates on a strict three-pillar model: 1. Saturday Advance Preparation via a dedicated Sunday Work (SUN) Tile for daylight permits (PTW-001..009 with Night Shift barred); 2. Sunday Zero-Creation Lockout where all permit creation is strictly blocked system-wide; and 3. Sunday Monitored Execution & Closure where pre-cleared permits can be safely executed and surrendered within their valid window.
Advance Window: Saturday Only (Zero Creation Allowed on Sunday) Permitted Scope: 9 Daylight Modules (PTW-001 to PTW-009) Night Shift Barred: Night Shift (PTW-010) Strictly Prohibited on Sundays Sunday Closure: Permitted within Valid Scheduled Window by Certified Supervisor
